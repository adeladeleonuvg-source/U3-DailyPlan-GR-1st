Unit 3 | Simple Present of Want May 20 - May 24 (Week 1, Class 1)

Simple present of want: I want / You want

CNB Competency & Learning Achievement: Expresses personal preferences and desires using simple affirmative structures orally and in writing, based on Our World 1 (SB pp. 100-101, WB p. 69).

Leader in Me Habit

Habit 2: Begin with the End in Mind (Setting goals for what we want to learn and do).

Moment I: Introduction & Vocabulary Warm-up (~5 minutes)

Brain Activation

Vocabulary 1 & 2 Review (Our World 1)

Flashcard review using images from SB pp. 96-97: a truck, a car, a kite, a ball, a drum, a train, a puzzle, a doll, a teddy bear. Teacher holds up flashcards or points to board; students call out the words enthusiastically.

Dynamic / Brain Gym

Song & Chant: Sing snippet from "Let's Play" (SB p. 99): "Do you want to play? Do you want to play with me? Yes, I do! Yes, I do!" Students stretch and act out playing with a ball or driving a train.

Moment II: Individual Work & Practice (20 minutes)

Self-Production

Student Book & Workbook Guided Practice

Students work individually on Student Book pages 100-101 and Workbook page 69. Complete sentences matching pictures with "I want..." or "You want...". Focus on neat handwriting and correct spelling.

Answers SB p. 100-101:
1. I want a train. / 2. You want a doll.
Answers WB p. 69:
Check matching lines: Boy with kite -> "I want a kite." Girl with teddy bear -> "You want a teddy bear."

Moment III: Cooperative Team Work (30 minutes)

Team Roles & Product

Pair / Small Group Mission

Working in pairs with assigned roles (Speaker and Card Holder). Students take turns showing a toy flashcard and stating: "I want a [toy]" or asking partner "Do you want a [toy]?" Partner answers "Yes, I do" or "No, I don't."

Leader in Me Habit

Habit 4: Think Win-Win & Habit 6: Sinergize (Listening to partners and sharing learning materials nicely).

Moment IV: Plenary, Interactive Quiz & Home Research (15 minutes)

Consolidation

Interactive Trivia / Game

"Pass the Toy" Mini-Quiz: Teacher holds a toy. Student must say: "I want a [toy]" to receive it, then pass it to a classmate asking "Do you want a [toy]?"

Take-Home Research / Activity:

Look in your toy box or bedroom at home. Find 2 toys and practice saying in English to your parents: "I want a [toy name]"!

Plenary Checklist (Teacher Evaluation)

Evaluation Criteria Yes (3) Progress (2-1)
1. Domain of target grammar & clear pronunciation
2. Correct structure usage ("I want...", "You want...")
3. Collaborative teamwork & active participation
4. Response to plenary questions & engagement
